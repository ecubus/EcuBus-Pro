## JSON-RPC

While the EcuBus GUI is running, other programs can access the live CAN devices over JSON-RPC (default `127.0.0.1:17320`). Frames those programs write appear as **Tx** in the trace.

Click **Apply RPC** after changing host or port. See [CLI JSON-RPC](../cli/rpc.md) for the protocol.
